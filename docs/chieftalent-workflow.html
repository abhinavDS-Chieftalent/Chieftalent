How ChiefTalent works

From a job description to a hiring decision in six stages. The AI runs the first round and recommends. People make every decision.

YouRecruiter or admin
ChiefTalentThe platform and the AI
CandidateIn their own browser
  1. 1

    Add the job

    Paste the job description or upload a PDF.

    Set the shortlist line. The default is 70 out of 100.

    Turns the job description into a clean structure and keeps a copy you can download.

    Not involved yet.
  2. 2

    CVs come in

    Upload as many PDFs as you have.

    Scores every CV against the job and says why.

    Catches exact duplicates and holds their interview link until you have looked.

    Not involved yet.
  3. 3

    Good fits get a link

    Send a link to anyone the AI turned down, whenever you want a second look.

    Above your line: a personal interview link is emailed.

    Below it: nothing is sent.

    Receives a link that belongs to them alone.

  4. 4

    The interview

    Nothing to do. Results fill in as it happens.

    Writes the 20 questions from the job and the candidate's own CV.

    Watches the session with integrity checks:

    Second personPhone in frameWhere they lookHead positionBackground voicesPhoto or screen spoofing

    Warns on leaving full screen or switching tabs. A third warning ends the interview.

    Confirms their email with a 6-digit code, shares a few details that are not scored, and checks camera and microphone.

    Then 30 minutes: one video answer and 20 multiple-choice questions.

    Finishes with a situation-based video question, answerable in about five minutes: working with others, technical thinking, problem solving, decisions and communication.

  5. 5

    The AI evaluates

    Waiting for the evidence page.

    Marks the multiple-choice answers.

    Turns speech into text on the platform, so accuracy never depends on the candidate's browser.

    Scores the spoken answers from the transcript and reviews how each one was delivered, with video and voice analysis.

    Recommends progress, review or reject, and gives its reasons.

    Finished.
  6. 6

    You decide

    Open one page with the recordings, transcripts, answers and AI notes.

    Select, reject or ask for another look. Overrule any recommendation, or issue a fresh interview.

    Keeps every score next to its reasons, so each recommendation can be checked.

    Finished.

Where a person decides

  • Which job, and where the shortlist line sits.
  • Whether a candidate the AI turned down still gets a link.
  • Whether to progress, reject or look again after the evidence.
  • Whether to issue a fresh interview.

Data and access

What is stored
CVs, interview answers, recordings, transcripts and AI evaluations.
What goes to the AI API
CV text and the transcripts of spoken answers, to score them and to write interview questions.
Deleting a candidate
Removes their CV, answers, recordings and evaluation.
Access
Staff sign in as an admin or a recruiter. Each interview link is unique to one candidate and needs an emailed code.
