HOW CHIEFTALENT WORKS
=====================

From a job description to a hiring decision in six stages. The AI runs the
first round and recommends. People make every decision.

Who is involved
  You            The recruiter or admin.
  ChiefTalent    The platform and the AI.
  Candidate      Takes the interview in their own browser.


STAGE 1: ADD THE JOB
--------------------
You
  - Paste the job description or upload a PDF.
  - Set the shortlist line. The default is 70 out of 100.
ChiefTalent
  - Turns the job description into a clean structure and keeps a copy you can
    download.
Candidate
  - Not involved yet.


STAGE 2: CVS COME IN
--------------------
You
  - Upload as many PDFs as you have.
ChiefTalent
  - Scores every CV against the job and says why.
  - Catches exact duplicates and holds their interview link until you have
    looked.
Candidate
  - Not involved yet.


STAGE 3: GOOD FITS GET A LINK
-----------------------------
You
  - Send a link to anyone the AI turned down, whenever you want a second look.
ChiefTalent
  - Above your line: a personal interview link is emailed.
  - Below it: nothing is sent.
Candidate
  - Receives a link that belongs to them alone.


STAGE 4: THE INTERVIEW
----------------------
You
  - Nothing to do. Results fill in as it happens.
ChiefTalent
  - Writes the 20 questions from the job and the candidate's own CV.
  - Watches the session with integrity checks:
      * a second person in the room
      * a phone in frame
      * where the candidate is looking
      * head position
      * background voices
      * photo or screen spoofing
  - Warns on leaving full screen or switching tabs. A third warning ends the
    interview.
Candidate
  - Confirms their email with a 6-digit code.
  - Shares a few details for the hiring team. These are not scored.
  - Checks camera and microphone.
  - Then 30 minutes: one video answer and 20 multiple-choice questions.
  - Finishes with a situation-based video question, answerable in about five
    minutes. It shows how they handle a real work situation: working with
    others, technical thinking, problem solving, decisions and communication.


STAGE 5: THE AI EVALUATES
-------------------------
You
  - Waiting for the evidence page.
ChiefTalent
  - Marks the multiple-choice answers.
  - Turns speech into text on the platform, so accuracy never depends on the
    candidate's browser.
  - Scores the spoken answers from the transcript and reviews how each one was
    delivered, with video and voice analysis.
  - Recommends progress, review or reject, and gives its reasons.
Candidate
  - Finished.


STAGE 6: YOU DECIDE
-------------------
You
  - Open one page with the recordings, transcripts, answers and AI notes.
  - Select, reject or ask for another look.
  - Overrule any recommendation, or issue a fresh interview.
ChiefTalent
  - Keeps every score next to its reasons, so each recommendation can be
    checked.
Candidate
  - Finished.


WHERE A PERSON DECIDES
----------------------
  - Which job, and where the shortlist line sits.
  - Whether a candidate the AI turned down still gets a link.
  - Whether to progress, reject or look again after the evidence.
  - Whether to issue a fresh interview.


DATA AND ACCESS
---------------
What is stored
  CVs, interview answers, recordings, transcripts and AI evaluations.
What goes to the AI API
  CV text and the transcripts of spoken answers, to score them and to write
  interview questions.
Deleting a candidate
  Removes their CV, answers, recordings and evaluation.
Access
  Staff sign in as an admin or a recruiter. Each interview link is unique to
  one candidate and needs an emailed code.
